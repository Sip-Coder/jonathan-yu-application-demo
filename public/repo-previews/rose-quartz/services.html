Featured specialty

Prenatal comfort massage

Side-lying and bolstered work for expectant mothers who need careful support without aggressive pressure. Sessions can focus on hips, low back, feet, shoulders, jaw, sleep comfort, and the emotional decompression that pregnancy often asks for.

  • Best after provider guidance and a trimester-aware intake.
  • Uses pillows, side-lying support, slower transitions, and pressure check-ins.
  • Can combine with gentle lymphatic pacing or quiet nervous-system reset.
Common length
60 or 90 minutes
Pressure
Light to medium, comfort-first
Best fit
Pregnancy comfort, hips, low back, feet, neck, jaw, or emotional rest.

Light-touch specialist care

Nervous-system reset

Slow, feather-light, consent-led work for guests who brace under typical massage pressure. This pathway is for burnout, grief, overwhelm, stress sensitivity, and anyone who needs the room to feel safe before the body can soften.

  • Begins with clear pressure boundaries and silence preferences.
  • Uses still contact, slow holds, breath-led pacing, scalp, neck, shoulders, and sacrum support.
  • Designed to feel therapeutic without forcing the body to defend itself.

60 or 90 minutes
Pressure
Light, slow, nervous-system safe
Best fit
Sensitive bodies, stress bracing, grief, burnout, and guests who prefer quiet.

Focused relief

Head, jaw, and headache care

Careful work for the scalp, temples, jaw, neck, shoulders, and upper back. This pathway is useful for guests who clench, carry screen tension, wake with jaw tightness, or want focused care without a full deep-pressure session.

  • Can include scalp, face, jaw, occipital, neck, and shoulder work.
  • Pressure stays specific and adjustable.
  • Pairs well with quiet breath pacing or guided check-ins.

Gentle flow work

Lymphatic flow

Light rhythmic touch for guests who feel heavy, swollen, sluggish, or travel-worn. This is not aggressive pressure. The work is slow, directional, and calming, with special attention to comfort and medical cautions when they apply.

  • Can support comfort around feet, ankles, legs, arms, face, or whole-body heaviness.
  • Useful as a gentle add-on to prenatal comfort when appropriate.
  • Guests should share surgery history, infection, clotting concerns, or provider guidance before booking.
Common length
60 minutes
Pressure
Very light and rhythmic
Best fit
Heaviness, travel fatigue, gentle recovery pacing, pregnancy comfort, and sensitive bodies.

Flexible therapeutic care

Restorative therapeutic massage

A customized session for guests who want relief, mobility support, sleep support, or focused work on muscular tension while still preserving Rose's consent-first, light-touch foundation.

  • Can include Swedish, myofascial-style pacing, focused pressure, or broad full-body work.
  • Best for hips, low back, shoulders, posture, and whole-body restoration.
  • Pressure can move between light, medium, and focused as the body allows.
Common length
90 minutes
Pressure

Best fit
General tension, mobility, posture, sleep, and clients who want one flexible session.

Water and breath ritual

Energy-grounding ritual

A longer, quieter session for guests who want massage to feel like a full return to self. The ritual pathway can include breath, stillness, warm grounding, optional aromatics, scalp work, and extra transition time.

  • Built for people who want care to feel ceremonial but still practical.
  • Works well after grief, burnout, major life transitions, or prolonged stress.
  • Pairs naturally with the Rose Quartz theme of compassion, warmth, and gentle return.

Adaptive gentle care

Special populations

A gentle framework for seniors, highly sensitive guests, caregivers, post-event recovery, and clients who need mobility support or careful pacing. Rose adjusts the session around comfort, position, pressure tolerance, and recovery needs.

  • Can be adapted for shorter sessions, quieter rooms, or more guided transitions.
  • Guests with medical complexity should bring provider guidance when appropriate.
