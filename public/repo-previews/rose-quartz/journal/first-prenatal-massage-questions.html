Pregnancy Comfort

Questions to ask before your first prenatal massage

A good prenatal session starts with clear questions about safety, positioning, pressure, and what the guest needs to feel protected.

Prenatal First visit Questions

Question one: how will I be positioned?

ACOG notes side-lying as a good position for massage while pregnant. Rose can explain how pillows, bolsters, and transitions will protect the belly and reduce strain.

This question helps the guest imagine the session before arriving, which can reduce anxiety.

Question two: how will pressure be checked?

Prenatal pressure should never be a one-time conversation. The body can change throughout the session, especially around hips, feet, ribs, and low back.

Rose can explain that light touch is a valid therapeutic choice, not a downgrade.

Question three: what should delay booking?

The guest should know which symptoms require provider guidance first. This makes the site more trustworthy and helps Rose avoid turning massage into a medical decision.

A careful no can be part of an excellent client experience.

Ask before booking

  • Are you trained for prenatal positioning?
  • Can pressure stay light?
  • What symptoms need provider guidance?
  • Can I choose silence?
  • Can pillows be adjusted during the session?
Guests with high-risk pregnancy, severe symptoms, unusual swelling, bleeding, dizziness, or provider restrictions should ask their clinician before scheduling.
