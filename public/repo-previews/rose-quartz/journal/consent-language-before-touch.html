Consent and Comfort

Consent language before touch: simple words that make massage safer

Guests should not need expert language to ask for comfort, pause, less pressure, or a different plan.

Consent Communication Safety

Clear words reduce guessing

Many guests endure discomfort because they do not want to interrupt. Rose can solve that before the session begins by giving simple language: lighter, same, more focused, pause, skip, or explain first.

When the words are already offered, using them feels less confrontational.

Consent is active care

Consent is not paperwork. It is the ongoing rhythm of how the session responds to the guest's body.

Rose can check pressure without making the guest manage the whole appointment. She can also watch breath, jaw, hands, and posture for signs the body needs a different pace.

The website can teach the room

A booking form that includes consent language prepares the guest to speak and prepares Rose to listen. It makes the session feel safer before touch begins.

That is a brand differentiator: clear, elegant, practical care.

Useful words

  • Lighter
  • Pause
  • Skip this area
  • More support
  • Explain first
  • Quiet please
Consent can be changed at any time. A guest can pause, redirect, or stop a session without needing to justify the decision.
