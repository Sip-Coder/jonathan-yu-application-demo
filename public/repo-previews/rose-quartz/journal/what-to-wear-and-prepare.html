First Visit Guidance

What to wear, bring, and expect before a Rose Quartz session

Clear preparation helps the body arrive with less uncertainty and gives Rose better information before touch begins.

First visit Preparation Comfort

Preparation should reduce pressure, not add it

Guests do not need a perfect routine before massage. The useful preparation is simple: arrive a little early, hydrate normally, wear comfortable clothes, and know what you want Rose to avoid.

The website can make this clear so first-time clients are not secretly worrying about etiquette.

Clothing and draping can be discussed

Many massage sessions use professional draping, but comfort varies by guest and service. Rose can explain what will stay covered, what areas are included, and how the guest can request changes.

For some light-touch or nervous-system sessions, the guest may prefer more clothing or less exposure. That preference belongs in the intake.

What Rose needs to know

The most important preparation is honesty: pressure sensitivity, pregnancy, recent procedures, headaches, swelling, dizziness, injuries, emotional tenderness, and scent preferences.

When Rose knows the context, the session can feel less like a generic appointment and more like care designed around the person arriving.

Before you arrive

  • Hydrate normally
  • Avoid rushing in
  • Bring provider guidance if relevant
  • Name scent sensitivity
  • Choose quiet or guided tone
Guests should share relevant medical history, recent injuries, pregnancy, surgery, infection, clotting concerns, and symptoms that may require provider guidance.
