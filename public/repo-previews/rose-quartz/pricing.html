After the first visit

Recurring care starts with a recommendation, not a contract.

Rose uses the first session, Rose Session Map, and aftercare response to suggest a return rhythm only when continued care makes sense.

  1. 01Map the first sessionStart with clear one-time pricing and the session length that fits your body today.
  2. 02Notice the responseUse aftercare notes to track what settled, what stayed tender, and what needs more time.
  3. 03Choose a cadenceRose may suggest weekly, biweekly, monthly, or as-needed care after she sees how your body responds.

No surprise base-price add-ons

Pressure consent, pathway matching, comfort intake, and aftercare are part of every listed session price. If an option is not listed on this page, use the one-time rate as the source of truth.

What is included

Every session includes Rose's comfort intake, pressure consent, service-pathway recommendation, and practical aftercare. Specialty pathways do not carry separate base-price surprises.

When to choose longer

Choose 90 or 120 minutes when more than one pathway fits, when the body needs slower transitions, or when prenatal, lymphatic, headache, and restorative goals need to be held together.
