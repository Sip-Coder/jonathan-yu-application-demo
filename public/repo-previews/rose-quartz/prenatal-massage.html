Prenatal massage in Las Vegas

Comfort-first prenatal massage for a body doing important work.

Pregnancy can make ordinary massage decisions feel loaded: position, pressure, swelling, hips, low back, feet, jaw, and emotional rest all matter. Rose Quartz frames prenatal care as a calm, side-lying, consent-led session with enough structure to feel safe before you arrive.

What to expect

Side-lying, bolstered, and easy to pause.

Rose uses pillows, careful draping, slow transitions, and pressure check-ins. Sessions can combine prenatal comfort with gentle lymphatic pacing, head and jaw care, or quiet nervous-system reset when appropriate.

Before

During
Ask for less pressure, a skipped area, silence, guidance, or a position change anytime.

Provider guidance

Prenatal care stays inside massage scope.

Massage is supportive care, not diagnosis or medical treatment. Unusual swelling, severe headache, dizziness, bleeding, blood-pressure concerns, fever, sudden pain, or high-risk pregnancy notes should be reviewed with a medical provider before massage.
